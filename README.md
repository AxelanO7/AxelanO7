Started freelancing in 2019, led a 114-member student organization as president, grew into a Mobile Engineering Lead, then Senior Mobile Engineer, and now serve as **CTO at New Directions Success**, architecting **Guestlist** — a digital ecosystem connecting tourism, lifestyle, entertainment, and premium service providers across web, mobile, backend, and back-office platforms.

- 🔭 Currently architecting **Guestlist** (marketplace platform) and AI initiatives like **Lerka**

- 🌱 Deep in **AI agents, RAG pipelines, and workflow automation**
- 👯 Open to collaborate on **scalable systems & AI integrations**
- 🎓 B.IT, Information Data Management — INSTIKI (GPA 3.62/4.00)
